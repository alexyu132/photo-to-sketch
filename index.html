Methods & reference implementations. Weighted-Voronoi stippling and Lloyd relaxation: Secord, Weighted Voronoi Stippling (NPAR 2002) — paper (PDF), as implemented by StippleGen and surveyed at dahtah.github.io/imager/stippling. TSP art: Bosch & Kaplan / Bosch & Herman; the tour stack here (Lloyd-relaxed points, nearest neighbour, 2-opt + Or-opt, uncrossing) follows the recipe described at lakolam.com/generators/tspart. Layered/local hatching behaviour is modelled on Plotcaster’s hatch and its LBG stipple; error diffusion follows Floyd & Steinberg (1976) — see Plotcaster’s Floyd–Steinberg notes. Flow streamlines use Jobard & Lefer’s evenly-spaced streamline placement over an edge-tangent field, the same family as DrawingBotV3’s Streamlines PFMs and vpype’s flow imager; spiral and squiggle-family variants are catalogued in mitxela’s plotterfun.
